This app selects name, aliases, WGS84 coordinates, timezone, country, population
and identifier fields and compresses them for offline search. It does not request
GeoNames services at runtime. Names and Chinese aliases come from the source.
City locations are city centers, not the user's position or a street address.
Data is provided without warranties. No endorsement by GeoNames is implied.
See geonames-provenance.json for source checksum and verified Chinese aliases.
